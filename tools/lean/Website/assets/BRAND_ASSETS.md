# Third-party brand assets

These files are embedded without visual modification in the Crest product site:

- `testflight-official.png` — TestFlight app artwork published by Apple on the official TestFlight public-invitation site: <https://testflight.apple.com/images/testflight-iOS-400x400_1x_40.png>
- `testflight-icon.webp` — dark TestFlight app artwork retained for the matching hero button.
- `reddit-official.png` — Reddit conversation-bubble Snoo mark published on Reddit's official brand page: <https://redditinc.com/brand>
- Discord mark (inline in `index.html`) — the Discord logo from Simple Icons (CC0), used only to link to the Crest Discord server.
- `crests/*.svg` — Crest Studio's nine Space templates, drawn from the app's crest geometry and palettes. The figures are game-icons.net artwork by Lorc under CC BY 3.0 (credited in the site footer and `CrestShared/Resources/HeraldicArtworkCredits.txt`). The Sun and River figures and Meadow's laurel trim are SF Symbols in the app and are redrawn here as plain shapes.

The surrounding labels and destinations identify the third-party service without implying sponsorship or affiliation.
