# Clear website data

Website data belongs to the Space that stored it, and it stays on this device. Clearing it in one Space never touches another Space, your saved passwords, extensions, downloaded files, or site permissions.

## Clear one site

Open Site Controls beside the address and choose:

- **Clear Cookies** removes the site's cookies and everything else it stored in the Space: local storage, IndexedDB, Cache Storage and service workers, including what sites embedded in it stored there. It signs you out of the site.
- **Clear Cache** removes only the responses Crest kept to load the site faster.

Crest clears the whole site, such as `example.com` and its subdomains, in both engines, reloads the page, and shows the result at the top of the window. The same actions are in the command palette as **Clear Cookies for This Site** and **Clear Cache for This Site**, and on iPhone and iPad in the page menu. In a private window, they clear only what that window holds in memory.

## Manage a Space's website data

On Mac, open Settings, select the Space, then select **Privacy**. On iPhone and iPad, open **Settings → Privacy** and choose the Space. **Website data** lists the sites that keep data in the Space and what each keeps. Choose **Remove** for one site, or **Remove All…** for every site in the Space.

## Clear browsing data

Below Website data, **Clear browsing data** removes what the Space browsed within a time range, from **Last Hour** to **All Time**. Choose any of **History**, **Cookies and Site Data**, and **Cached Images and Files**, then **Clear…**. Clearing history in a Space that syncs removes it from your other devices too.
