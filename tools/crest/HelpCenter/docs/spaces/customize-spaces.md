# Customize and arrange Spaces

On Mac, the rounded Space selector sits between the window controls and Back, Forward, and Reload. Each Space uses a small color dot. Select a dot to switch Spaces; the Space's name remains available in its tooltip and accessibility label.

## Edit a Space

Right-click a Space in the selector and choose **Edit Space**, or select the Space in Settings. Change its **Space name** or choose one of six colors: blue, teal, green, amber, coral, or purple. Colors stay the same in light and dark appearance.

## Add, arrange, and delete Spaces

Right-click a Space in the selector for **New Space**, **Edit Space**, and **Delete Space**. New Spaces receive an available color automatically, which you can change in Settings.

Use the Space order controls in Settings to rearrange Spaces. Their order also determines **Control-1** through **Control-9**.

Deleting a Space removes its tabs and browser profile data. Downloaded files stay on disk. Crest keeps at least one Space.
