# Community code of conduct

Crest welcomes people who want to improve the browser, review its security, or
help other users. Participate with curiosity, patience, and respect.

Expected behavior includes giving actionable technical feedback, assuming good
faith while still challenging claims with evidence, respecting privacy, and
making room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation, and publishing another person's private browsing or account
information are not acceptable. Do not use project spaces to promote malware,
credential theft, deceptive browser builds, or other abuse.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban an
account when necessary to protect contributors and users. Report sensitive
conduct concerns through a private GitHub Security Advisory and label the
report as a community-safety matter.
