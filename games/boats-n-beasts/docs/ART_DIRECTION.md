# Direction B — tactile procedural 3D

The selected reference is [direction-b-tactile.png](visual-restart/direction-b-tactile.png). It supersedes the earlier flattened concept passes. The reference is art direction only and is never loaded as a game asset.

## Shared scene contract

- Engine-independent C# simulation retains its coordinates and rules. The Godot adapter maps `(x,y)` to `(x/100,0,y/100)` with Y up; boat bows point along local -Z.
- Orthographic camera: 0.74 pixels per simulation unit, 0.84 ground-plane vertical foreshortening. The same camera projects HUD anchors and unprojects sailing clicks onto the water plane.
- Warm upper-left sun, cool ambient fill, matte materials, softened cast and contact shadows. Deep petrol sea, irregular subdued turquoise shallows, warm sand, sage/olive vegetation, rounded stone, cream cottages and wood boats.
- Gunboat, Aura and Mage retain distinct equipment; Mage crystal stands vertically. Crab faces, claws and shells and the other monsters must read as rounded solid forms at gameplay scale.
- Position-history wakes curve naturally. Surf and magic stay restrained. Fish look submerged and have no overhead icon. Avoid glowing shore halos, concentric ring ornament, repeated water blobs, flat cutouts and boxy buildings.
- Every world mesh/material/animation is generated by C# or Godot shaders. No external art packs, handcrafted imported models, image-generated gameplay assets, photographs or GDScript.

## Baseline and acceptance

[direction-b-baseline.png](../evidence/direction-b-baseline.png) and its telemetry record the native pre-restart game. Ranked gaps: (1) flat actor and structure silhouettes/materials, (2) uniform water and outlined shores, (3) sparse/blocky architecture and vegetation, (4) weak consistent lighting/contact, (5) graphic encounter/effect symbols.

First evaluate the separate `art-sample.tscn` native scene: boat, crab, cottage, island and water, using the production camera and lighting. It contains no voyage simulation or injected gameplay state. F12 captures normal scale; Tab toggles an inspection view. Then integrate and compare normal-scale native gameplay to B. A build or an inventory of added details does not establish visual acceptance.

Final acceptance remains pending until the combined native scene has independent critique without major unresolved gaps, preserves actual gameplay, passes Debug and Release builds, and demonstrates sustained performance targeting smooth 60 FPS. Reject or revert visual regressions instead of accumulating compensating tweaks.
