**Source verdict: accepted, with no blocking implementation finding.** Runtime movement, save round trips and exported gameplay require the separate flight review; this report does not substitute for it.

- Forward+ is explicit in project settings, OpenGL fallback is disabled, and no mobile or desktop preset overrides reinstate Compatibility. Startup queries the actual method/driver, disables processing and exits before save loading or world construction on a wrong renderer. The retained source log reports `forward_plus / metal / Apple M3 Max (Apple9)`. The independent forced-OpenGL log reports the intended requirement error without the earlier downstream null-object error. An explicitly overridden editor itself is outside this game-startup guard.
- The helper implements the exact sRGB piecewise transfer. Custom shader vertex pigments and authored palettes are converted once; pine `source_color` samples are already linear and are not decoded again. StandardMaterial vertex conversion remains enabled. Water vertex red is generated slope data and is correctly excluded from color conversion. Shader palette modulation before decoding preserves the existing artistic pigment treatment.
- Native four-sample MSAA and alpha coverage replace the screen-coordinate foliage discard pattern. The [.20 scissor + .14 antialias edge behavior](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/godotengine/godot/4.7/servers/rendering/renderer_rd/shaders/forward_clustered/scene_forward_clustered.glsl) was independently checked in Godot 4.7's renderer implementation: the engine adds these thresholds before coverage evaluation. Texture-size coordinates are supplied. Camera reveal remains intentional, excludes shadow passes, and uses the swayed position. Coverage is not proof of shimmer-free animation.
- Water is an opaque terrain-following sheet 7.5 cm above the same sampled ground. Its normalized ripple perturbation transforms a world-space direction through the view matrix with zero homogeneous translation. Dielectric specular response and rough banks replace the old painted glints. There is no remaining depth sampler requiring reverse-Z repair, no hidden OpenGL depth convention, and no second obsolete transparent-water implementation.
- The brush shader, UID and installation function are deleted; the former shadowless fill is removed. Searches found no executable reference to them. Historical evidence mentioning Compatibility remains correctly historical. Procedural fur filtering, palette variation, soft-foliage reveal and separate CanvasLayer UI remain useful artistic/gameplay techniques. Runtime resource paths remain local to this game.
- The diff leaves flight physics, camera behavior, input mappings, progression/scoring, collision, terrain/section generation and seeded obstacle placement, save format and save/load routines unchanged. Crown rendering geometry changes only through removal of the obsolete opaque filler cores described below; both needle sprays and their RNG sequence remain. New review settings are opt-in transient rendering controls. The generated UID files do not add gameplay behavior.

**Visual assessment:** the stream pair demonstrates a material improvement: a broad restrained reflected response replaces the almost black channel while bank obstacles remain distinct. These images do not support claiming that every color or fine edge improved.

Residency differs appreciably from earlier isolated sessions for reasons not established by this review; the delta cannot be assigned precisely to the effects or the foliage correction. These short measurements do not establish indefinite residency or performance on another GPU. ## Follow-up: close canopy finding

The independent flight reviewer subsequently supplied three canopy flight frames at 113–123 m. Inspection reveals conspicuous near-black triangular wedges amongst the needled sprays. Source inspection traces these to the four opaque filler triangles emitted per crown arm with negative sentinel UVs and very dark pigment. This is a substantive visual defect missed by the initial lower staged views. Recommended removing those triangles and their shader sentinel branch while retaining both angled needle sprays, followed by close-canopy movement and distant-crown review. The removed block has no random-number calls or collision role; its removal leaves placement and RNG sequence intact. The lead removed exactly this block and the shader sentinel branch. Independent reinspection of corrected `core-woods-flight-a/b/c.png` at 110–120 m shows the black wedges gone, much cleaner close foliage, and the squirrel, ground opening and solid trunks still distinguishable. `core-after-summit.png` retains recognizably full distant crowns and the three-route composition; there is no concerning thinning in this view. The corrected source contains no remaining core sentinel path. **Finding closed; correction accepted.** The supplied sequence samples moving play rather than certifying every intervening frame or all camera speeds.
