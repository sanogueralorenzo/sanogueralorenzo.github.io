# Rendering and removal review — 6 September 2026

Reviewed the final project/script/shader diff, desktop presets, launch instructions, verification notes, original visual target and refinement findings, and the before/after woods650, deep-woods2300 and stream820 PNGs. This review used source and captured images; no game was launched and no automated tests were created. The reviewer previously implemented the pigment helper and initial foliage changes, so those checks are not independent of their implementation. The separate flight/visual review is needed for independent runtime confirmation.

No blocking source defect was found in the reviewed migration.

## Confirmed in source

- `project.godot` selects Forward+, removes the Compatibility mobile override, disables automatic OpenGL fallback, and declares Godot 4.7. All three desktop presets inherit it without renderer overrides. The startup guard queries the actual renderer and driver, prints them with the adapter, disables processing and exits with status 1 on an unsupported renderer before loading saves or creating gameplay objects. This guards game execution; a separately launched editor can still be explicitly overridden by the user.
- All authored spatial palettes and custom-shader vertex pigments pass through the exact sRGB transfer. Pine color samples use `source_color` and are not decoded twice. StandardMaterial vertex-color decoding remains enabled. Water `COLOR.r` remains numerical slope data. Sky conversion uses the same exact transfer. HUD drawing is unchanged and separate from 3D antialiasing.
- The brush shader, UID, installation function and canvas nodes are deleted. No live references remain. Historical evidence correctly still names the removed shader. The screen-coordinate foliage dither and handwritten needle discard are gone; their replacements are the sole shipped implementations. The shadowless directional fill is deleted, with the replacement ambient balance owned by the environment.
- Pine coverage uses 4× MSAA, alpha scissor `.20`, coverage edge `.14`, and pixel-space texture coordinates. Godot adds the two thresholds before derivative-based coverage, giving a nominal `.34` contour; mip compensation means this is not a promise of identical silhouettes at every distance. Camera reveal remains limited to soft foliage and is excluded from shadow passes. Sway is included in the world-position varying. These semantics agree with the [official 4.7 shader implementation](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.7-stable/servers/rendering/renderer_rd/shaders/forward_clustered/scene_forward_clustered.glsl).
- Water is an opaque dielectric with world-space ripple normals transformed into view space. Retaining an opaque sheet is consistent with the unchanged terrain-following geometry at a 7.5 cm offset. No existing depth sampler was left with OpenGL conventions, and no fabricated deep-water absorption or redundant transparent implementation was added. Sky reflections cannot reflect nearby offscreen trees; SSR was explicitly evaluated and rejected.
- Changes to `game.gd` are renderer enforcement, atmosphere, filter removal and opt-in diagnostics. Flight model, input handling, camera movement, generation, collision, progression, scoring, HUD, sound and save format have no implementation diff. Shader dependencies use this game's `res://` paths; Cozy Sora is research material, not a runtime dependency. Desktop presets exclude development evidence and do not introduce external asset requirements.

## Visual judgment and limits

The matched stills show softer directional shadow edges, more tonal variation on the squirrel, and a clearer reflected-sky response in the stream. Trunks, rocks, route openings and the HUD remain readable in the inspected frames. The palette retains olive vegetation and chestnut fur.

This is a restrained rendering improvement, not a transformation into the reference's dense rocky ravine. Fine pine cards still show conspicuous high-contrast stipple and jagged detail, and mineral contours remain graphic. Woods650 makes the squirrel darker than the before image, though its body and membrane remain distinguishable. The fog adds a light veil and reduces contrast slightly. The stills do not prove absence of foliage shimmer, fog jitter, ghosting or gameplay obscuration during all movement.

The comparison matches geometry, camera and fixed sunlight; unlocked cloud, foliage and particle phases prevent pixel-exact matching, and the animated sky can change irradiance. Report the documented timing windows as measured evidence, not a locked-60 claim: p95 exceeds 16.67 ms and RSS increases. Native export execution, sustained play, saves and movement findings belong to the separate runtime reports. This review does not independently certify those measurements or other GPUs/operating systems.
